# Art and fonts

## Original vector artwork

Created specifically for this personal continuation:

- `mascot.svg`: Pip, a friendly explorer robot with a sprout antenna.
- `bug.svg`: an original smiling bug used as a game target and selectable Lab character.
- `levels.svg`: original illustrations for all five game modes.

No Scratch/MIT characters, logos, artwork, block outlines, or brand assets were copied. The supplied reference guided mood, outdoor scenery, tactile controls, and level presentation rather than being reproduced as a webpage.

## Adventure landscape

`adventure-world.png` was generated using the built-in image-generation tool, then inspected and copied into this repository. It is background art only; all text, controls, and gameplay are real HTML/CSS/JavaScript. No AI service is called by the app.

Final generation prompt:

> Use case: stylized-concept
> Asset type: original website hero background for a coding adventure for Years 7–12 students.
> Primary request: Create one bright, polished cartoon adventure game environment illustration. Wide landscape composition, approximately 1536x1024. A pale blue sky with a few soft puffy clouds near the outer edges, leafy trees framing both the left and right edges, small floating grass islands in the far right distance, and a meandering sandy footpath through rolling green hills occupying the lower half. Small wildflowers and rounded rocks accent the grassy foreground.
> Style/medium: original hand-painted 2D adventure game art, appealing rounded shapes, softly outlined details, gentle brush texture, sophisticated friendly storybook game finish.
> Composition/framing: generous clean pale blue sky across the upper and middle center for live HTML title overlay. Keep the center sky uncluttered. Trees frame rather than block the center. The path curves from the bottom foreground toward the hills near the center horizon. Wide immersive scenery, no panels or borders.
> Lighting/mood: warm sunny daylight, inviting and adventurous.
> Color palette: saturated cheerful greens, soft cyan blue sky, warm sandy cream path, restrained colorful flowers; cheerful but not neon.
> Constraints: landscape only; no text, letters, numbers, UI, buttons, logos, watermark, characters, brand assets, or Scratch assets. Everything must be original.
